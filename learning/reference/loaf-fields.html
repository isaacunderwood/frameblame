Every LoAF Field, and What It Excludes

What each field measures, in which clock, and the thing it quietly leaves out. Built for reading beside a live trace.

The frame

All timestamps are performance.now() milliseconds since the document's timeOrigin, coarsened onto a randomised 100 µs grid (5 µs when cross-origin isolated). Treat any difference under ~0.2 ms as noise.

Name your denominator

duration stops at paintTime. In measured frames presentationTime landed a further 23–42 ms later. The same 45 ms of component work is ~70% of duration and ~45% of keystroke-to-pixels. State which one you mean, every time.

Three rules

Scripts under 5 ms are not reported. A frame with no script entries is not a frame with no JavaScript.

Nested entry points are merged. Once a frame has a pending script, a new one is not started — which is why an entire React render arrives as one entry rather than a tree.

forcedStyleAndLayoutDuration is nested inside the script's own duration. Adding it as a separate slice double-counts.

On browser-work-dominated frames the unattributed slice reached 93%. Cross-check it against the share of profiler samples with an empty JavaScript stack: the two agreed closely (93% ↔ 100%, 7.7% ↔ 1.9%), which turns "we don't know" into a measurement rather than a shrug.
